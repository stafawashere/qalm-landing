4. Prayer times, Qibla and alerts

  • Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculations. They depend on the method, offsets and location you choose, your device clock and its sensors. They can differ from your local mosque, from other apps, and from a printed timetable. If your mosque or your scholar gives a different time, follow them.
  • In some places, such as high latitudes and Turkey, the calculation is an approximation and the app says so.
  • Alerts are scheduled on your device and are subject to your device settings, battery state, Focus modes, silent switch, the permissions you have given and limits set by Apple. I cannot promise an alert will fire in every circumstance. Do not rely on Qalm as your only way to catch a prayer time that matters to you.
  • The Qibla compass relies on your device's sensors and can be wrong near metal or magnets. Check against the map view and local knowledge.
  • Nothing in Qalm is a fatwa, a ruling or religious advice.
  • The qada estimator gives a rough count from the numbers you enter. It is an estimate, not a ruling on what you owe, and you can change every number. Schools of thought differ on how missed prayers are made up, so ask a scholar you trust.
