About Qalm

Qalm is a prayer times and Quran app, coming to iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac. It shows the daily prayer times for where you are, the direction of the Qibla, and the Quran in Arabic with a translation. Qalm is built to collect nothing: no accounts, no ads, no analytics. It is made and published by Mahfuj Mustafa as an individual.
